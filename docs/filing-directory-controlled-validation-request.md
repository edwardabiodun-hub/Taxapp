# Controlled filing-workflow validation request

## Purpose

This request is for an official revenue-service representative or an authorized test-account owner to confirm whether a jurisdiction supports online individual Personal Income Tax (PIT) or Form A submission.

The result must be evidence of an actual workflow, not only a portal description, payment facility, tax registration page, employer PAYE function, or general self-assessment statement.

## Scope

Validate one jurisdiction at a time, beginning with:

1. Bauchi
2. Imo
3. Kaduna

The same checklist can be used for Bayelsa, Delta, Sokoto, Yobe, and other jurisdictions later.

## Required workflow evidence

Please provide screenshots or a screen recording with sensitive values redacted, or conduct a live support-assisted walkthrough showing:

1. The official portal URL and the revenue authority identity.
2. The taxpayer category selected, specifically **individual**.
3. The return type, specifically **Personal Income Tax**, **individual annual return**, or **Form A**.
4. The assessment year available in the workflow.
5. The income, deduction, or supporting-document sections presented to the individual taxpayer.
6. The final submission action.
7. The resulting submission acknowledgement, reference number, receipt, or visible workflow status.
8. Whether payment is required before submission, after assessment, or through a separate process.
9. Whether the workflow is available for the current filing period.
10. The date of validation and the name or role of the validating reviewer.

## Exclusions

Do not submit a real taxpayer return. Do not provide passwords, TINs, NINs, BVNs, payment-card details, or other personal taxpayer information.

An observation counts only as supporting evidence—not as confirmation—if it shows only:

- taxpayer registration or TIN issuance;
- payment or invoice generation;
- tax-clearance verification;
- employer PAYE or employer annual returns;
- generic direct-assessment or tax-obligation language;
- an unauthenticated marketing claim without the underlying workflow.

## FileSmart decision rule

FileSmart may promote a public filing-directory status only after the workflow evidence is complete, the evidence is current for the relevant filing period, and a second reviewer independently confirms the interpretation. Until then, both online-tax-submission and individual-PIT-submission statuses remain `not_verified`.
